# Holdfast

Holdfast keeps notes on the user's Mac and helps them find, import, and review information.

## Language

**Model installation**:
A downloaded model whose required files have been checked and are ready for use.

**Download follow-up**:
The user's requested action after a model installation, such as selecting the answer model or preparing meaning search. It remains pending until the app can accept it, including after an explicitly resumed download.

**Loaded answer model**:
An answer model held in memory for inference. Keeping its downloaded files and keeping it loaded are separate choices.

**Listen source**:
The audio input to a listening session, which can end naturally, fail, or be stopped by the user.

**Verified release**:
A release whose installer matches its recorded identity and passed verification checks. Verification describes particular artifact bytes, not merely a version name.
