# Holdfast v0.0.4

Signed and notarized by Apple, with easier model management.

- Browse, download and manage models in one window, with progress, pause and resume.
- Compare answers from several models using the same question and sources.
- Attach files to a chat without adding them to your notes.
- Choose a search reranker and try the new Parakeet speech model options.

Parakeet Redux transcription is not yet verified; its first load can take a long time.
Your notes, settings and downloaded models stay in place when upgrading.
