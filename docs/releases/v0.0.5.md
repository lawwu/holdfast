# Holdfast v0.0.5

Listen to YouTube and other video links.

- Paste a YouTube, Vimeo, Twitch or SoundCloud link into **Listen → A stream** to transcribe it on your Mac. Requires `yt-dlp` and `ffmpeg`.
- Transcripts use the video's title. Unavailable videos and broken streams now show useful errors.
