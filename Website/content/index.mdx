## A home for what you know

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="Find the right passage" icon="magnifier">
    Search across your notes and documents with keywords or meaning. Answers include sources you can open and check.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Keep the final say" icon="approve-check">
    Ask for an edit and review the proposal in your Inbox. Apply it when you’re ready, with a saved copy available for Undo.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Choose where answers run" icon="laptop">
    Use LocalMLX inside the app, or connect Ollama, LM Studio, or a compatible server. Protected notes stay on this Mac; server providers receive the other context they need.
  </Card>
</CardGrid>
