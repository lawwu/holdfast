## Follow along

1. [Install Holdfast](/holdfast/getting-started/#1-install-holdfast) and choose an answer model.
2. Unzip the [sample notes](/holdfast/demo/holdfast-sample-notes.zip). In **Settings → Vault**, choose the **Holdfast Sample Notes** folder.
3. Open **Library → Kitchen repair plan** to see the starting note.
4. In **Ask**, enter **What did I decide about the kitchen repair?** Then open a citation to check its source.
5. Start a new question: **Mark “Book the inspection” as done in my home checklist.**
6. Open **Inbox**, review the red and green text, and choose **Apply Change**.
7. In **Recently filed**, choose **Undo** to restore the saved copy.

The sample vault contains four short notes: a kitchen repair plan, a home checklist, team notes, and a weekend plan. You can safely experiment with this copy before choosing your own folder.

## Transcript

The narration below matches the video. The voice was generated with ElevenLabs; the answers and edit proposal came from Holdfast running a local model.

### 0:00 — Your notes, within reach

Welcome to Holdfast, a home for your notes on your Mac. In this short tour, we'll find a decision, check its source, and make a change that you approve. Everything shown here uses fictional sample notes.

### 0:13 — Choose where answers run

Start by choosing your notes folder and an answer model. In Settings, LocalMLX is the default: it runs the model inside Holdfast on your Mac. Download a model that fits your available memory. You can also connect Ollama, LM Studio, or a compatible server. A server receives the context needed for your request.

### 0:34 — A familiar place to browse

The Library lets you browse your notes and read them in the app. Here is our kitchen repair plan, with a budget and a few decisions. Your notes remain ordinary files. You can keep using your favorite editor alongside Holdfast.

### 0:48 — Ask a question in plain words

Open Ask and type a question your notes can answer. Let's ask: What did I decide about the kitchen repair? Holdfast searches the notes and answers with sources. This answer was generated by a local model. We've removed the waiting time from this tour; the time you'll need depends on your Mac and model.

### 1:07 — Check the source

Open a numbered citation to read the source passage beside the answer. Here, we can check the repair choices and the eighteen hundred dollar budget against the original note. Treat the answer as a helpful starting point, and use the source to check the details.

### 1:22 — You have the final say

Now let's update the checklist. Ask: Mark Book the inspection as done in my home checklist. Holdfast prepares a proposal in the Inbox. The file hasn't changed yet. Review the removed text in red and the replacement in green. You can edit the proposal before applying it.

### 1:41 — Apply only when you're ready

Choose Apply Change to save it. Holdfast first saves a copy, then updates the note. Only the inspection checkbox changes. If someone has edited the note since the proposal was made, Holdfast refuses to overwrite it, so you can review a fresh proposal instead.

### 1:57 — Room to change your mind

Changed your mind? Open Recently filed in the Inbox and choose Undo. Holdfast restores the saved copy. We checked that this sample checklist returned exactly to its original contents. Review, apply, and undo all stay under your control.

### 2:13 — Try it with the sample notes

You're ready to try it. Download the sample notes from the tour page, choose that folder in Holdfast, and ask the same kitchen question. Then try the checklist edit. The getting started guide walks you through installation, models, and your first question.
