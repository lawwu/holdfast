# Kitchen repair plan

## The decision

Repair the cabinet doors and replace the leaking tap. Keep the existing countertop.
The total budget is $1,800. We chose a warm white finish for the cabinet doors.

## What happens next

Book the inspection before ordering parts. Aim to finish the work before the December break.
The action list is in [[home-checklist]].

## Why this approach

The cabinet frames are sound. Repairing the doors keeps the project within budget
and avoids replacing a countertop that still works well.
