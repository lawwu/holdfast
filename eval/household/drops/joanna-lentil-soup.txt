JOANNA'S RED LENTIL SOUP (serves 6, about 40 minutes)

2 tbsp olive oil
1 onion, 2 carrots, 2 celery stalks, chopped
3 cloves garlic
1 tsp cumin, 1 tsp smoked paprika, 1/2 tsp turmeric
1 1/2 cups red lentils, rinsed
1 can (14 oz) diced tomatoes
6 cups chicken or vegetable broth
juice of 1 lemon, salt and pepper, spinach (a couple handfuls)

1. Saute onion, carrot and celery in the oil 8 minutes. Add garlic and spices for 1 minute.
2. Add lentils, tomatoes and broth. Simmer 25 minutes until the lentils fall apart.
3. Blend half of it with a stick blender, stir in spinach and lemon juice.

My kids like it with a swirl of yogurt and crusty bread. Freezes great!
