Case Report — Session 4
(SYNTHETIC TEST DOCUMENT — counselee and family are fictional)

Counselee: Rebecca Sandoval
Date: 2026-10-22 · Session: 4 · Length of Session: 60 minutes

What can you highlight as significant background information?
No new background. Rebecca's husband Tomas Sandoval did not attend this time.

Give a summary of reason(s) the counselee came for counseling—the presentation problem(s).
Same as before: anxiety, panic at the August retreat, fear of man.

What was gained by performing the homework from last session? What change?
Rebecca made the call to her mother-in-law with Tomas beside her. She told her they would
not host Thanksgiving this year and offered to bring two pies. Her mother-in-law was quiet
and short on the phone but called back two days later to ask what kind of pie. Rebecca
read chapters 1-4 of Welch and prayed aloud once in small group. No panic episodes in four weeks.

What was your planned agenda for this session? Was it accomplished? If not, why not?
Hear how the call went; begin addressing the fear beneath the panic attacks. Accomplished.

What main problems were discussed in this session?
The fear that if she is not liked she is not safe; how this traces back to her mother.

What Biblical solutions were presented in this session? What Scripture?
Psalm 34:4 ("I sought the LORD, and he answered me and delivered me from all my fears").
Romans 8:15, a spirit of adoption, not of fear.

What specific applications to the counselee's life did you discuss from your Scripture?
That her safety rests in being God's adopted daughter, not in others' approval.

What are you seeing (or not) about the counselee's faith in who God is, about their relationship with Jesus, and/or about the role of the Gospel in their daily life and problem?
She is beginning to speak of God as Father rather than evaluator.

What unbiblical habits of thinking and/or behaving (preconditioning issues) are you seeing in the counselee?
Still over-explains when anxious, but less.

What ruling thoughts, desires, and/or convictions of the heart are emerging or still present?
Desire for approval, weakening.

What homework was given and how did it specifically apply to problems?
1. Memorize Psalm 34:4.
2. Read Welch chapters 5-8.
3. Write a letter (not to send) to her mother about how her criticism shaped her fear.

If someone asked the counselee right after the session, "What did you learn that you needed to change, with God's help?" what would you want him to say? What challenge did you give?
"I am safe because I am God's daughter." Challenge: the letter.

How was hope or encouragement given in this session?
Four weeks without panic; the phone call went better than she feared.

How is the overall counseling process progressing? What issues have been sufficiently addressed by you and are changing in the counselee?
Very encouraging. Fear of man with her mother-in-law is changing.

What is your goal for the next session? Why? If you are past the 3rd session, does it follow your Tentative Counseling Plan?
Discuss the letter to her mother; yes, follows the plan (session 6 is the planned wrap-up).
