# Swedish meatballs (Farmor's recipe)

Source: Erik's grandmother. Serves 6. The family's potluck standby.

## Ingredients

- 1 lb ground beef, 1/2 lb ground pork
- 1/2 cup breadcrumbs soaked in 1/2 cup milk
- 1 egg, 1 small onion grated, 1/2 tsp allspice, 1/4 tsp nutmeg, salt and pepper
- Sauce: 3 tbsp butter, 3 tbsp flour, 2 cups beef broth, 1/2 cup heavy cream, 1 tbsp soy sauce

## Steps

1. Mix the meatball ingredients and roll into 1-inch balls (about 40).
2. Brown in batches in butter; set aside.
3. Make a roux with butter and flour, whisk in broth, cream and soy sauce; simmer until thick.
4. Return meatballs to the sauce and simmer 15 minutes.

## Notes

Serve with mashed potatoes and lingonberry jam. Doubles well for potlucks — use the big Dutch oven.
