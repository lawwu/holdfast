# Sheet-pan chicken fajitas

Source: Anna, adapted from a magazine clipping. Serves 5. Weeknight dinner.

## Ingredients

- 1.5 lb chicken thighs, sliced thin
- 3 bell peppers and 1 large onion, sliced
- 2 tbsp oil, 2 tsp chili powder, 1 tsp cumin, 1 tsp smoked paprika, 1 tsp salt, juice of 1 lime
- Tortillas, sour cream, salsa, avocado

## Steps

1. Heat the oven to **425°F**.
2. Toss chicken and vegetables with oil and spices on a large sheet pan; spread in one layer.
3. Bake **22 minutes**, stirring once halfway.
4. Squeeze lime over everything; serve in warm tortillas.

## Notes

Soren eats his "plain" — pull some chicken before the spices. Leftovers make good quesadillas.
