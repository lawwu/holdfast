# Case Report — R.S. — Session 2

SYNTHETIC case report for testing. R.S. and her family are fictional.

Date: 2026-09-24 · Session: 2 · Length of Session: 60 minutes

## What can you highlight as significant background information?

No new background. She attended small group on 2026-09-16 and spoke once.

## Give a summary of reason(s) the counselee came for counseling—the presentation problem(s).

Same as session 1. She had one milder episode of panic in the church parking lot on a Sunday but went in anyway.

## What was gained by performing the homework from last session? What change?

The worry journal had 31 entries in one week. Reading it together, 22 of them were about what someone would think of her: most often E.S., then the small-group women, then other moms at school pickup. She read Matthew 6 daily and went to small group.

## What was your planned agenda for this session? Was it accomplished? If not, why not?

Review the journal, teach the fear of man, introduce the fear of the Lord as the alternative. Accomplished.

## What main problems were discussed in this session?

Fear of man: approval-seeking with E.S., who has asked her to host Thanksgiving this year; R.S. said yes immediately and has dreaded it since. Also her silence in small group.

## What Biblical solutions were presented in this session? What Scripture?

Proverbs 29:25: "The fear of man lays a snare, but whoever trusts in the LORD is safe." Isaiah 51:12-13: who are you that you are afraid of man who dies?

## What specific applications to the counselee's life did you discuss from your Scripture?

We traced the Thanksgiving yes: she said yes because she feared E.S.'s disappointment, not because it was wise for her family. The snare is that the fear she thought would protect her now controls her calendar.

## What are you seeing (or not) about the counselee's faith in who God is, about their relationship with Jesus, and/or about the role of the Gospel in their daily life and problem?

She was moved by Isaiah 51 and said, "I've made people bigger than God." She is beginning to see that she is already accepted in Christ.

## What unbiblical habits of thinking and/or behaving (preconditioning issues) are you seeing in the counselee?

People-pleasing; mind-reading what others think; saying yes before asking T.S.

## What ruling thoughts, desires, and/or convictions of the heart are emerging or still present?

Desire for approval, especially from older women; desire to avoid conflict at any cost.

## What homework was given and how did it specifically apply to problems?

1. Memorize Proverbs 29:25.
2. Continue the worry journal, adding a column: "What does God say about this?"
3. Talk with T.S. about whether hosting Thanksgiving is right for their family this year.

## If someone asked the counselee right after the session, "What did you learn that you needed to change, with God's help?" what would you want him to say? What challenge did you give?

"I've been more afraid of people than of God." Challenge: talk to T.S. before saying yes to anything new.

## How was hope or encouragement given in this session?

Isaiah 51:12-13 and her own insight. Her honesty about the journal was a real step.

## How is the overall counseling process progressing? What issues have been sufficiently addressed by you and are changing in the counselee?

Encouraging. She is engaged and doing the work.

## What is your goal for the next session? Why? If you are past the 3rd session, does it follow your Tentative Counseling Plan?

Teach the fear of the Lord more fully and help her prepare to speak honestly with E.S.

## Thinking back on/listening to this session, what could you have done better?

I should have asked more about how T.S. responds when she says yes to E.S.; I don't yet know his part.
