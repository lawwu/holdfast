# Fitness goal

**Erik: 3 runs and 2 strength sessions every week. That's the whole goal.**

Set in January 2026 after a season of chasing race times that had become more about pride
than health. The goal is consistency, not performance: no race calendar, no pace targets,
no mileage ramps. A week counts as "met" when it has at least 3 runs and 2 strength sessions
(Monday–Sunday weeks). Sunday family walks are welcome but don't count toward the goal.

- Runs: two easy weekday runs (3–4.5 mi) and one longer Saturday run (5.5–7.5 mi).
- Strength: Monday and Friday, 35–45 minutes, at home.
- Missed weeks happen (travel, sickness). Don't make up for them by doubling the next week.

Anna's goal is simpler: a walk most days and a Saturday hike with the kids when the weather allows.
