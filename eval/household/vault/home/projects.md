## Back fence replacement (planned, spring 2027)

The cedar fence along the back and north side (about 160 linear feet) is 25 years old,
leaning in two places and patched after last winter's windstorm.

### Quotes

| Contractor | Quote | Includes | Timing |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Cedarline Fence | $8,900 | 6 ft cedar, steel posts, tear-out and haul-away, permit | can start March 2027 |
| Pacific Fence Co | $7,450 | 6 ft cedar, wood posts, tear-out; we haul away old fence | February 2027 |
| DIY (Erik + brother Nils) | about $4,200 materials | everything ourselves | four or five Saturdays |

### Decision

Going with **Cedarline Fence at $8,900**. Steel posts should outlast wood, and they handle
the permit and haul-away, which saves us several Saturdays. Erik's Saturdays are better
spent with the kids than on a post-hole digger. Paying from the home-projects savings
bucket (see [profile](../finance/profile.md)). Deposit due when we sign in January 2027.
