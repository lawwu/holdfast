## Rules

- Never record employer-confidential material: customers, internal numbers, unreleased plans, or colleagues' performance and pay.
- Decisions are weighed in this order: motive, our walk with God, family and church, then the role itself and money.
- Add reflections as dated sections at the bottom of [notes.md](notes.md).
