# Case Report — R.S. — Session 3

SYNTHETIC case report for testing. R.S. and her family are fictional.

Date: 2026-10-08 · Session: 3 · Length of Session: 60 minutes

## What can you highlight as significant background information?

T.S. attended the first 20 minutes at my invitation. He said he had not realized how much the Thanksgiving request was weighing on her.

## Give a summary of reason(s) the participant came for support—the presentation problem(s).

Same. No panic episodes in the last two weeks.

## What was gained by performing the homework from last session? What change?

She memorized Proverbs 29:25 and said it in the car before small group. The journal's new column helped: she wrote Scripture next to about half of the entries. She and T.S. talked and decided not to host Thanksgiving this year.

## What was your planned agenda for this session? Was it accomplished? If not, why not?

Prepare her to tell E.S. no about Thanksgiving, and teach the fear of the Lord. Accomplished, though T.S.'s part took longer than planned.

## What main problems were discussed in this session?

How to say no to E.S. graciously; her fear that E.S. will be cold to her and the children; T.S.'s habit of staying out of it.

## What Biblical solutions were presented in this session? What Scripture?

Proverbs 9:10, the fear of the LORD is the beginning of wisdom. Ephesians 4:15, speaking the truth in love. Assigned Ed Welch's "When People Are Big and God Is Small," chapters 1-4.

## What specific applications to the participant's life did you discuss from your Scripture?

We role-played the phone call to E.S.: thank her, say plainly that they will not host this year, offer to bring a dish, and not over-explain. T.S. agreed to be beside her for the call.

## What are you seeing (or not) about the participant's faith in who God is, about their relationship with Jesus, and/or about the role of the Gospel in their daily life and problem?

She prayed aloud at the end of the session, briefly, without being asked. She said afterward that she was nervous but that "God was bigger than my nerves."

## What unbiblical habits of thinking and/or behaving (preconditioning issues) are you seeing in the participant?

Over-explaining and apologizing; assuming E.S.'s reaction before it happens.

## What ruling thoughts, desires, and/or convictions of the heart are emerging or still present?

Approval is still the main one, but she is naming it herself now.

## What homework was given and how did it specifically apply to problems?

1. Make the call to E.S. this week, with T.S. present, and write down what happened.
2. Read "When People Are Big and God Is Small," chapters 1-4.
3. Pray aloud once in small group in the next two weeks.

## If someone asked the participant right after the session, "What did you learn that you needed to change, with God's help?" what would you want him to say? What challenge did you give?

"Saying no to E.S. can be loving, and God's approval is already mine in Christ." Challenge: make the call this week.

## How was hope or encouragement given in this session?

Her prayer at the end of the session and the two weeks without panic.

## How is the overall private process progressing? What issues have been sufficiently addressed by you and are changing in the participant?

Good progress. The worry journal and Proverbs 29:25 are taking hold; the conversation with E.S. is the next test.

## What is your goal for the next session? Why? If you are past the 3rd session, does it follow your Tentative Private Plan?

Hear how the call went and continue in Welch; begin addressing the fear underneath the panic attacks directly.

## Thinking back on/listening to this session, what could you have done better?

I let T.S. stay quiet; I should have asked him directly how he will support her when E.S. pushes back.
