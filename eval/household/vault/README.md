# Halvorsen household knowledge base

SYNTHETIC test fixture. The Halvorsen family and everyone in this vault are fictional.

## Purpose

We are a Christian family in Alder Creek, Oregon: Erik (43), Anna (41), and our
three children Leif (12), Greta (9) and Soren (5). Our aim is to love God with all
our heart, soul, strength and mind, and to love our neighbors as ourselves. The
money, the house, our health and our calendar are stewardship in service of that,
never ends in themselves.

This vault is our family assistant's memory. Answer questions from what is written
here, and when something new happens, record it in the place the domain README says.

## Domains

Each domain's `README.md` is its operating manual: what lives where, a "where to look"
table, and the rules for recording new information. Read it before changing anything.

| Domain | What it holds | Start at |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `finance/` | Household profile, giving, insurance, net worth, the open-items checklist, transaction and net-worth CSVs | [finance/README.md](finance/README.md) |
| `home/` | The house: maintenance log, one note per major appliance, planned projects | [home/README.md](home/README.md) |
| `health/` | Fitness goal, clinic and dental visits for every family member, activity log | [health/README.md](health/README.md) |
| `life/` | Trips, recipes, the family calendar | [life/README.md](life/README.md) |
| `career/` | Erik's work and the decisions around it | [career/README.md](career/README.md) |
| `private/` | Erik's biblical-private case reports (initials only) | [private/README.md](private/README.md) |

## Conventions

- Today's date for this fixture is **2026-11-05**; timezone America/Los_Angeles.
- Dates are written YYYY-MM-DD.
- Never record account, card, policy or member numbers anywhere in this vault.
  Institution names and balances at most.
- CSVs under `<domain>/data/` are the source of truth for numbers; notes are the
  source of truth for context and decisions.
