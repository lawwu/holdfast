Local models on a family knowledge base

How well do models that run entirely on a Mac answer questions from a family's notes, and file new documents into them? Every model here runs on-device with MLX; nothing leaves the laptop.

What we found

  • Qwen 3.8 27B is the most accurate at everything tested, and the slowest: 74% of facts in plain answers, 100% on the hardest questions in Research mode, and every one of 12 documents filed in the right place.
  • Gemma 4 26B is nearly as good and 3–5× faster: 73% of facts at 11 s per answer, against 31 s for Qwen. It slips on harder work: 79% in Research mode.
  • The small Gemma 4 E4B answers simple questions as well as the big models, but when filing it kept two full names in a counseling report that should use initials only, and filed a duplicate.
  • Search quality comes from the reranker. Google's EmbeddingGemma ties Qwen's embedder once Qwen's reranker sorts the results; without it, the Google-only setup found fewer of the right passages and its answers fell to 59%.
  • Numbers need Research mode. No model can total a CSV from search passages; with tools to query the CSVs, Qwen answered all six spending and workout questions.

How this was run

The knowledge base is invented: a family of five with finance notes and spending CSVs, home maintenance logs, health visits and a workout CSV, trips, a career note, and biblical-counseling case reports that follow an initials-only rule. Today's date is fixed at 2026-11-05. Answers and filings are scored by text patterns, with no model judging another. Each setup ran once on MacBook Pro, M1 Max, 64 GB; with 40 questions and 12 documents, differences of a few points are noise.

Not yet run: Gemini as a cloud ceiling, and the next local candidates (Qwen 3.6 35B A3B, gpt-oss 20B, Gemma 4 31B, larger Qwen embedders and rerankers).
