IMPORTANT SAFETY INSTRUCTIONS

Read all instructions before using this appliance. This refrigerator must be properly installed and located in accordance with these instructions before it is used. Do not allow children to climb, stand or hang on the doors or shelves. Never clean refrigerator parts with flammable fluids. Keep fingers out of pinch-point areas; clearances between the doors and cabinet are necessarily small. Unplug the refrigerator before cleaning or making repairs. If the power cord is damaged, it must be replaced by the manufacturer, its service agent or a similarly qualified person. Do not use an extension cord. Child entrapment and suffocation are not problems of the past: junked or abandoned refrigerators are still dangerous. Before you throw away your old refrigerator, take off the doors and leave the shelves in place.

Read all instructions before using this appliance. (See also the Quick Start card included in the literature pack.)

INSTALLATION

Allow 1/2 inch of clearance on each side and at the top, and 1 inch behind the unit, for air circulation. Do not install where the temperature will fall below 55°F or rise above 110°F. The floor must be strong enough to support a fully loaded refrigerator, about 700 pounds. Level the refrigerator side to side and front to back by turning the leveling legs. The front should be slightly higher than the back so the doors close easily. Connect the water line using 1/4-inch copper or the supplied PEX tubing; do not use plastic tubing without a reinforced braid. Flush the water system by dispensing 3 gallons before first use. Discard the first three batches of ice.

Allow 1/2 inch of clearance on each side and at the top, and 1 inch behind the unit, for air circulation. (See also the Quick Start card included in the literature pack.)

USING THE CONTROLS

The control panel is inside the fresh-food compartment at the top. Recommended settings are 37°F for the fresh-food compartment and 0°F for the freezer. Allow 24 hours for temperatures to stabilize after any change. Turbo Cool lowers the fresh-food temperature quickly after a large grocery load and turns off automatically after 6 hours. Vacation Mode raises the fresh-food setting to 55°F to save energy; do not store perishable food while it is on. The Door Alarm sounds after the door has been open 60 seconds; press and hold Alarm for 3 seconds to silence it permanently.

The control panel is inside the fresh-food compartment at the top. (See also the Quick Start card included in the literature pack.)

THE ICE MAKER AND DISPENSER

The ice maker produces about 3.5 pounds of ice per day. It will not produce ice until the freezer reaches 10°F. If the ice maker does not run, check that the feeler arm is in the down position and that the water supply valve is open. Do not use the dispenser to crush ice cream or frozen food. Hollow or small cubes usually mean low water pressure or a clogged filter; replace the filter first. To reset the ice maker, press and hold the test button for 3 seconds. Empty the ice bucket before long absences.

The ice maker produces about 3.5 pounds of ice per day. (See also the Quick Start card included in the literature pack.)

STORAGE TIPS

Store meat and fish in the lower crisper set to Meat. Store vegetables in the upper crisper set to Produce. Butter keeps best in the covered dairy bin. Do not overload door bins; they are rated for 15 pounds each. Allow air to circulate around food. Hot food may be placed in the refrigerator after it has cooled for 30 minutes. Freezer: wrap food in moisture-proof material to avoid freezer burn. Do not refreeze thawed food.

Store meat and fish in the lower crisper set to Meat. (See also the Quick Start card included in the literature pack.)

CLEANING

Clean the interior every few months with a solution of 2 tablespoons baking soda in 1 quart warm water. Do not use abrasive cleaners, bleach or cleaners containing ammonia on the fingerprint-resistant stainless finish; use a soft cloth and mild dish soap. Vacuum the condenser coils behind the toe grille once a year, more often with pets. The door gaskets can be cleaned with mild soap and warm water; dry thoroughly. Shelves are tempered glass; let them reach room temperature before washing in warm water.

Clean the interior every few months with a solution of 2 tablespoons baking soda in 1 quart warm water. (See also the Quick Start card included in the literature pack.)

NORMAL SOUNDS

Your new refrigerator may make sounds your old one did not. A high-pitched whine or pulsating sound is the variable-speed compressor adjusting to cool efficiently. Clicking is the defrost timer or the water valve. Gurgling is refrigerant flowing. Popping is contraction and expansion of the inside walls. A buzzing sound may be heard when the water valve opens to fill the ice maker. Water dripping on the defrost heater can cause a sizzling sound. None of these sounds indicate a problem.

Your new refrigerator may make sounds your old one did not. (See also the Quick Start card included in the literature pack.)

TROUBLESHOOTING

Refrigerator does not operate: check the plug, the household fuse or breaker, and whether the unit is in Showroom Mode (press Freezer and Fresh Food together for 5 seconds to exit). Temperature too warm: the doors may be opened too often, the door gaskets may not seal, or the coils may be dirty. Frost or ice crystals on frozen food: the door may have been left ajar. Water on the floor: the drain pan may be cracked or the water line connection loose. Water has poor taste: replace the filter. Dispenser slow: low household water pressure (below 40 psi) or a clogged filter. If the problem persists, call Corvell Service before scheduling any outside repair; unauthorized repairs may void the warranty.

Refrigerator does not operate: check the plug, the household fuse or breaker, and whether the unit is in Showroom Mode (press Freezer and Fresh Food together for 5 seconds to exit). (See also the Quick Start card included in the literature pack.)

WATER FILTER

Use only genuine Corvell CWF-9 filters. Replace the filter every 6 months, or sooner if
the filter indicator turns red or water flow decreases. To replace: turn the old filter a
quarter turn counter-clockwise, pull out, insert the new filter and turn clockwise until it
locks. Dispense 2 gallons to flush. Reset the indicator by holding Filter for 3 seconds.
