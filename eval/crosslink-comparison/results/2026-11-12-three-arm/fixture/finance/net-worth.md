# Net worth — narrative

Month-end numbers are in `data/net-worth.csv` (cash, investments, home equity, debts).
This note explains the movements; read the CSV for the figures themselves.

- Most of the growth is investments (Evergreen brokerage, Roth IRAs, Erik's 401(k)),
  helped by steady contributions and a good market in late spring and summer.
- **March 2026** was the one real dip: the market fell and investments dropped for the
  month even though we kept contributing.
- Home equity grows slowly — about $1,100 a month of principal; we do not mark the house
  to market more than once a year.
- Debts are only the Odyssey car loan, falling about $400 a month.
- Cash rose through the year as the emergency fund finished filling in spring and the
  home-projects bucket for the [[home/projects|back fence]] started.

What we want to remember: none of this is the point. A larger number is only more to
steward and more to give from (see [giving](giving.md)).
